## Causal formula, frozen (no optimizer)
- D1 TREND: EMA20 above EMA50 and last fully CLOSED D1 close above EMA20 and EMA20 above its value 5 complete D1 sessions back => LONG; inverted SHORT. Evaluate at 4H event end using most recent D1 candle **whose END <= candidate end**, not next/current daily close. EMA initialized with SMA20/SMA50; require at least 80 completed historical D1 days.
- 4H ATR: trailing 14 complete 4H true ranges (including only history prior to event, no future), arithmetic SMA. For LONG a prior impulse from 4H close at k-11 to k-3 >= 1.5 ATR(k-1). Exactly two 4H pullback closes c(k-2)<c(k-3), c(k-1)<c(k-2), and FIRST recovery c(k)>c(k-1). Pullback extreme min(low[k-2],low[k-1]); depth from c(k-3) 0.4–2.5 ATR. SHORT mirrored. Require fully closed 4H k and daily trend same direction. No separate extra 15m indicator; this experiment tests 1H information content only.
- Entry at next 4H OPEN = 1H bar aligned to the next 4h boundary. Stop at correction extreme including 4H recovery candle k, offset 0.3 ATR(k-1), for LONG below min 4H lows (k-2:k), for SHORT above max 4H highs; positive stop distance <= 5 ATR; skip otherwise and count. No TP; follow existing trend via one-way 4H 3-bar structural trailing stop (last 3 fully closed 4H lows/highs ± 0.3 ATR), recalculated only at NEXT 4H open. 1H intrabar adverse touch exits with gap at next available open if through stop. Stop-first conservative. No invented force_exit on study end, report censored/unrealized separately.
- 1H FAILED COUNTERINITIATIVE: among 12 fully completed 1H candles ending at or BEFORE k 4H close, at least one t (with prior 6 1H candles available) breaks the minimum of 6 preceding 1H lows (LONG) / maximum prior 6 highs (SHORT), **closes back** within prior 6h range, and at least one subsequent 1H closes beyond t's high (LONG) / below t's low (SHORT) before or at decision time. All known before entry; no value from future hour, volume or indicators needed. Otherwise UNCONFIRMED. This is different from old failed contact_close V3 3H after-entry criterion, which lost money; do not retest V3.
- Candidate non-overlap: group all 4H candidate events chronologically within each symbol; after each selected candidate wait until its exit or end; no within-symbol overlapping exposure. This selection is independent of confirmation flag and outcome, because availability is determined by the prior entry's objectively observed exit (operational baseline). Report symbol and UTC-week clustering to avoid false 24-times independence.
- Cost sensitivity: roundtrip 0.40% notional BASE and 0.65% STRESS (taker+spread/slippage pessimistic proxy). Actual funding settled from original futures/um fundingRate series while holding, signed by side and included in net. REAL orderbook fills, historical maintenance-margin/mark-driven liquidation NOT independently reconstructed; never VERIFIED. Stop/slippage proxy must be labeled.
