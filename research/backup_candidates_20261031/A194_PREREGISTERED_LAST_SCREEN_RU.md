## Preregistered experiment: 3 causal, independently implemented BASLINES

**Important:** These **are new simple hypotheses**, not a falsely claimed original source or proven ready-made profitable strategy. Compared with historical source A193, they test primitive families under unified risk/execution. The file `a194_three_mechanics_fixed.py` and all hardcoded settings were committed **before any backtest CI was run**. No hyperparameter tuning after seeing results is allowed.

- **A. Trend Pullback:** 1h EMA50>EMA200 for longs (reverse for shorts), 15m EMA20>EMA50; previous 15m candle tags EMA20, current close reclaims in trend; avoid chase >0.8 ATR; volume ratio >=0.75. Entry next 5m open. SL below 3-candle 15m structural low or EMA50 minus 0.15 ATR (reverse for shorts).
- **B. Break + Retest:** 15m close outside preceding 20x15m highs/lows, volume >=1.3x, 1h EMA trend and 15m EMA20/50 aligned. Requires actual later candle retest of broken level within 6 closed 15m bars, volume>=0.7x, closes through level. Entry next 5m open. SL structural near retest last 3 candle extreme/level +/-0.15 ATR.
- **C. Liquidity Sweep:** current 15m low breaches previous20-candle low >=0.10 ATR, recovers inside on same close with bullish body and upper-close location >=0.60, volume>=1.0x (short inverted). No 1h filter because seeks distinct reversal family. Stop beyond rejection wick +/-0.15 ATR. Entry next 5m open.

**Control exit:** TP at 1.75 times entry-to-structural-SL distance, not claimed academic EMBER 4h target. This deliberately common *test control* allows comparing entry mechanisms apples-to-apples, and is **not** a replacement for 4h zone-to-zone TP/SL in main EMBER trading architecture. Max holding 8h. Stop distance 0.25–2.50% price (no trades outside). Hard stop, no averaging, no DCA or martingale.

**Risk & fees:** three independent model equity accounts $1,000 (each pair / strategy), risk budget1% each at structural stop, nominal position cap3× simulated account, optimistic fee 6bps per side + 2bps slippage per side, stress 12.5bps fee +5bps slippage per side. True entry at *next 5m bar OPEN* (not current close); intra-5m hits of SL/TP settled stop-first; first opened candle included. Both-side fee deducted from net trade PnL. **Funding is not modeled**, 5m wick order uncertainty and exchange metadata/real order books not resolved. Drawdown based on closed trades, underestimates mark-to-market drawdown.

**Source:** real Binance Vision USD-M BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT 5m monthly July–September2026, 2026-07 warmup, research test `[2026-08-01, 2026-10-01)` (61 days). Strict checks contiguous 5m candles, >=0 OHLCV volume, each monthly ZIP SHA256. 15m and 1h are resampled from same 5m source, 1h trend only from last completed full hour; no future shifted bars. This is a **retrospective frozen, no-retune test**, not truly untouched prospective post-publication OOS. The only statistically independent inputs are markets to a limited extent: BTC, ETH, SOL are highly correlated.

**Exploratory acceptance filter locked ahead of results:** for each strategy >=100 actual trades pooled across 3 pairs in 61d, positive PnL after cost and PF>=1.30 at base, positive PnL and PF>=1.15 at stress, at least 2 of 3 pairs net-positive under both fee scenarios, max closed trade DD per pair within 12% and no extreme top-3 dependence. Meeting these **does not** qualify for LIVE/PAPER auto execution: subsequent chronological out-of-sample other regimes+coins, 1m real stop sequences, funding, bid/ask simulated impact, true full-account DD and calibration are still required. If none passes, default action is **stop searching for random open-source lucky curves**, prioritize verifiable source and redesigned research from known elements, not loosen filters.

No downstream parameter optimizations permitted in this test. Never claim 3–5 trades/day will materialise unless observed and geographically/timefully robust.
