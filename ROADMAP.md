# EMBER Roadmap

## Phase 1 - Core Engine

- data engine, features and context builder;
- pullback and ignition setups;
- risk and future-only exit simulation;
- event-driven backtester;
- leakage tests and synthetic sanity demo.

## Phase 2 - Validation Layer

- dynamic portfolio and kill-switches;
- purged walk-forward with embargo;
- quality and completed-trade structure gates;
- Markdown, CSV and Parquet reports;
- parameter-grid research runner.

## Phase 3 - Paper Mode

- FastAPI and SQLite virtual journal;
- public Binance data integration;
- minimum 100 paper trades and 30 days;
- compare paper metrics with backtest expectations.

## Phase 4 - Live Gate

Blocked until Phase 3 and WFO requirements pass. Live execution is deliberately absent.
